# Cambridge International
Cambridge International offers an AS & A Level in Computer Science (9618), which is taken by students in many countries, often at international schools. (It isn't available to state schools in England, which use AQA, OCR or Eduqas.)

## AS & A Level (9618)
| Paper   | What it covers                                   | Format                                  | Length             | Marks | Weighting          |
|---------|--------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------|-------|--------------------|
| Paper 1 | Theory fundamentals                              | Written                                 | 1 hour 30 minutes  | 75    | 50% of AS, 25% of A Level |
| Paper 2 | Fundamental problem-solving and programming skills | Written, in pseudocode                | 2 hours            | 75    | 50% of AS, 25% of A Level |
| Paper 3 | Advanced theory                                  | Written                                 | 1 hour 30 minutes  | 75    | 25% of A Level     |
| Paper 4 | Practical                                        | On a computer, in Python, Java or VB.NET (console mode) | 2 hours 30 minutes | 75 | 25% of A Level |

- AS Level students take Papers 1 and 2. A Level students take all four, either in the same exam series, or Papers 1 and 2 in their first year and Papers 3 and 4 in their second.
- **Paper 2** answers must be written in **pseudocode**. Cambridge has its own pseudocode, which is quite different from Python (and from the AQA and OCR pseudocodes). It's defined in Cambridge's _Pseudocode Guide for Teachers_, which you can get from your teacher or from Cambridge's support site. Make sure you practise writing it: it's worth a quarter of the A Level!
- **Paper 4** is taken on a computer without internet or email access. You write complete programs in your chosen language, in "console mode" (i.e. text-based programs, without a graphical interface, like the ones in these lessons), and submit them along with evidence of testing.
